Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an assessment take?
Typically 2-6 weeks depending on organization size and complexity. Small orgs: 2-3 weeks. Medium: 3-4 weeks. Enterprise: 4-6 weeks. Timeline starts after initial consultation and scoping.
Will the assessment disrupt our operations?
No. We conduct non-intrusive assessments that don't impact production systems. Scanning happens off-peak hours. We work with your team to minimize any operational impact.
What if we're not ready for Caiber implementation?
The assessment is valuable regardless. You'll get a detailed roadmap you can use with any vendor. Many clients use our assessment, then implement with Caiber 6-12 months later based on budget/timeline.
Can we get samples of previous assessments?
We can share a redacted sample report and discuss findings from similar organizations (under NDA). Contact our team to request samples.
What happens after the assessment?
You get a detailed roadmap. Many clients choose Caiber for implementation, but it's optional. We're happy to discuss how Caiber can address your specific findings.
How is pricing determined?
Based on infrastructure size (systems count), complexity, and scope. Initial consultation is free. We'll provide exact pricing after scoping call.
